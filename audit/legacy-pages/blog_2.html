A Guide to Yam Cultivation for Maximum Yield

admin 13 Jul, 2025

Yam is a staple food in West Africa, and its successful cultivation is key to the livelihood of millions. This comprehensive guide covers everything from selecting the right yam setts to proper spacing, soil management, and pest control. Whether you are a small-scale farmer or a commercial producer, these tips will help you maximize your harvest.

Key Takeaways:

  • Soil preparation is crucial.
  • Timing of planting affects yield significantly.
  • Integrated pest management is more effective than chemical pesticides alone.
